- New: the Xion Client Mods menu. Press Right Shift in game and it opens straight on Mods
  - Every mod in your profile as an animated card with its own logo, cut clean from its background
  - Switch any mod on or off right there; the change applies the next time you press Play
  - The ⚙ button opens a mod's own settings (Sodium Extra, Iris, Zoomify, Motion Blur and more)
  - Filter by Xion Pack or the mods you added yourself; mods that others depend on stay on
- Mods you switch off stay off: the Xion Pack no longer downloads them again on the next launch
